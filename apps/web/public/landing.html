The problem

Investing laterusually means never

A salary can invest for you before you ever see the money. Freelancers, creators and anyone paid in lumps don't get that.

The money lands, bills come first, and investing moves to next month. Then the month after.

  • What if investing happened the moment money arrived?
  • What if it took one tap?
  • What if every payment counted, big or small?

How it works

Set it oncethen just get paid

  1. 01

    Set a rule once

    20% of every payment: 60% S&P 500, 40% Nvidia. Your wallet signs it, and it lives on Solana.

  2. 02

    Get paid

    From a client, a platform or a prize, in USDC. Stash sees it land in your wallet, so the amount is never typed in.

  3. 03

    Tap stash

    One transaction buys every stock in the rule. All of it lands, or none of it does.

Why Solana

What is actually built

Running on mainnet statewith test money

Stash runs on a copy of Solana mainnet. The routes come from Jupiter, and the pools and xStocks are the real ones. Only the balances are fake.
