How it works

Three steps. The last one is one tap.

  1. 01

    Set your rule

    Choose how much of each payment to keep and where it goes: one or two stocks. Your wallet signs it, and it lives on Solana.

    20% of every payment
    S&P 500 · 60%Nvidia · 40%
  2. 02

    Get paid

    A client, an employer or a hackathon pays you in USDC. Stash reads it off the chain, so the amount is never typed in.

    $500.00received · from A1Jd…oFtP
  3. 03

    Stash it

    One tap. Your wallet signs one transaction that buys the whole basket: every stock lands, or nothing moves.

Before you start

Your keys. Your stocks.

  • Your key stays with you. Stash builds each transaction; your wallet signs it; Stash checks it is byte-for-byte what it built before sending.
  • Not for everyone. xStocks are not available to US persons, or to people in Canada, the UK or Australia.
  • A 0.5% fee on each stock bought or sold, taken through Jupiter.
  • Test money for now. The demo runs on a copy of Solana mainnet: real pools and real stock tokens, with test balances.
