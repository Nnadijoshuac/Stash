Why Solana

A whole basket in one transaction.

Tokenized stocks exist on several chains. What Stash needs from Solana is narrower and measurable: several stocks, routed by Jupiter, bought atomically for a fraction of a cent, on a Sunday as easily as a Tuesday.

What's real

Real logic. Fake money.

  • Runs on a local copy of Solana mainnet. The pools, stock tokens and programs are real mainnet state. The money is fake, and nothing here costs anything.
  • Your key stays with you. Stash builds each transaction; your wallet signs it; Stash checks it is byte-for-byte what it built before sending.
  • Not for everyone. xStocks are not available to US persons, or to people in Canada, the UK or Australia.
  • A 0.5% fee on each stock bought or sold, taken through Jupiter.
